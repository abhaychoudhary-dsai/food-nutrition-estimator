How it works

1. Recognise

An EfficientNet-B0 CNN, pre-trained on ImageNet and fine-tuned on 20 dishes, looks at the photo. It runs as an ONNX model, so the server does not need PyTorch.

2. Look up

The dish is matched to a recipe in the Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB, 2024): 40+ nutrients per 100 g for 1,014 Indian recipes.

3. Scale

Values are scaled to your portion. The default is INDB's standard serving, which you can change in grams.

Be aware

  • It estimates from a standard recipe. Your home recipe, oil and ghee can change the numbers a lot.
  • For deep-fried dishes (samosa, pakode, bhature, burger) the source data counts all the frying oil, so the numbers are not shown.
  • Jalebi, momos and paani puri are recognised but are not in INDB yet.
  • Only one dish per photo. A full thali will be labelled as just one dish.
